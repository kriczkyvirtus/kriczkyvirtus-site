## 1 · HUB CLIPPING — approved, but narrow the fades rather than removing them

**Your diagnosis is better than my hypothesis.** I assumed the missing image was collapsing the layout; it's the two 60px edge-fade overlays sitting above the cards at `zIndex: 2`. That also explains why desktop is affected — I'd have kept looking in the wrong place.

**Approved, with one change.** Instead of suppressing the fades entirely at phone width, **narrow them to 20px**.

The arithmetic: at 390px the card is 340px, leaving roughly 50px of gutter. A 60px fade overruns the gutter and eats into the card. A 20px fade sits inside the gutter and never touches content.

Why narrow rather than remove: the fade is the only signal that more cards exist off-screen, and that signal matters *most* at phone width, where a single card fills the viewport and there's no other clue the carousel scrolls. Removing it makes the card look like the only one.

```
≤ 430px  →  fade width 20px
> 430px  →  unchanged at 60px
```
